# SWOT Analysis: Improved Star Rewards Loyalty Programme

This is a summarised, initial SWOT based on the [Project Brief](./ProjectBrief.md). It focuses on the proposed modernised loyalty programme, distinguishing BrightStar's current position from potential future benefits. Validate the assumptions with customers and relevant business teams.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| BrightStar already has an established loyalty programme and customer base to build on. | The current programme relies on physical cards, paper statements, manual form processing, and service-desk support. |
| BrightStar has direct customer feedback that clearly identifies pain points and desired improvements. | Points are batch-processed weekly rather than updated in real time, delaying balance visibility and driving support calls. |
| The company has a clear digital vision: improve loyalty, personalise customer engagement, and gain better insights. | Inconsistent, manually entered customer data limits reliable analysis and relevant offers. |
| Customers shop both in stores and online, providing a basis for a joined-up loyalty experience across touchpoints. | Current offers can be poorly matched to customers' interests, and reward redemption can be confusing for store staff and customers. |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|
| Introduce a digital membership experience, such as app-based access, to remove dependence on a physical card and paper statements. | Competing retailers already offer more dynamic digital loyalty experiences; customers may switch if BrightStar's experience does not improve. |
| Provide faster points and balance visibility, clearer reward redemption, and more relevant offers to improve engagement. | The target to show results within 12 months creates delivery and scope risk, particularly given the legacy mainframe and data issues. |
| Use improved customer data and timely insights to support personalisation and measure campaign effectiveness. | Poor data migration, inaccurate balances, or unreliable integrations could undermine customer trust in the new programme. |
| Reduce manual administration and avoidable customer-service contacts through streamlined digital processes. | Customers who do not use smartphones or prefer non-digital service could be excluded if existing alternatives are removed. |

## Summary

BrightStar can build on its existing programme and customer feedback to address clear shortcomings in convenience, timeliness, and offer relevance. The main opportunity is a seamless, dependable digital experience; success depends on improving data quality and legacy integration while delivering within the timeframe and serving customers with different digital preferences.
