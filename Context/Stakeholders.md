# BrightStar Project Stakeholders

The primary scope is modernising the Star Rewards loyalty programme. Returns and exchanges are an emerging operational challenge, but the project brief describes them as a focus for a later phase.

## Star Rewards loyalty programme

| Stakeholder | Likely interest or impact |
|---|---|
| Current Star Rewards members and customers | Need an easy-to-use digital loyalty experience, accurate and timely points, relevant offers, and simpler redemption. |
| Prospective and younger customers | A key audience for improving digital engagement and attracting new programme members. |
| Product Owner | Provides product context and is likely to shape priorities, requirements, and decisions for the loyalty solution. |
| Product Managing Director | Concerned about BrightStar's competitive position and the customer experience compared with other retailers' loyalty offerings. |
| Marketing Managing Director and marketing team | Want more app adoption and active purchasing; likely to shape personalised offers and campaign needs. |
| CTO and technology teams | Responsible for delivering a digital solution within the stated 12-month timeframe and addressing the limitations of the batch-processed mainframe. |
| Store managers, assistant managers, and cashiers | Use or support the loyalty and redemption processes in stores; need workable procedures and tools that reduce customer friction. |
| Customer service staff and helpline teams | Handle points queries and redemption problems; affected by delays and avoidable customer contacts. |
| Data, analytics, and loyalty operations teams (potential) | Likely to support customer-data quality, points processing, insight generation, and targeted offers. These teams are implied by the programme's data and batch-processing issues, but are not named directly in the brief. |

## Returns and exchanges (potential later-phase stakeholders)

If the work expands to returns, the brief also identifies store and operations managers, assistant managers, cashiers, customer service coordinators, the Finance Manager, and the Legal Manager. They have interests in reducing queues and manual work, applying return rules consistently, processing refunds accurately, managing inventory, and meeting the stated return policy.

This is a starting stakeholder list based on the [Project Brief](../ProjectBrief.md). Stakeholder responsibilities and decision authority should be confirmed with BrightStar.
